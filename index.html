EtsyPulse AI App brings shop performance and business expenses together to help its owner understand sales and make better marketing decisions.

How data is handled ↗

This is the application's information site. The shop dashboard is privately operated and has no public sign-up.

Purpose

A clearer view of the business

01 / Shop performance

Understand the evidence

Review Etsy sales, listings, traffic and imported advertising data alongside recorded expenses and data coverage.

02 / Receipt review

Keep costs organized

Import PDF receipts from an owner-selected Google Drive folder, extract expense details, and review uncertain values or possible duplicates.

03 / Marketing decisions

Turn evidence into ideas

Use configured AI services to suggest revenue opportunities and listing improvements for the owner's review.

Google Drive integration

Receipts and encrypted recovery copies

The receipt connection reads PDFs from the selected folder and its subfolders. A separate backup connection, when configured, stores encrypted database snapshots in a dedicated Drive folder and can download them for recovery checks.

Access follows the purpose

Receipt retrieval uses read-only service-account access. Backup authorization uses drive.file for files created or authorized for the backup app.
